About this world

Every organism carries its own randomly initialised neural network and mutates it when it splits. Organisms interact only through the 32-channel chemical field: they absorb it, are fed or poisoned by its composition, drift along its gradients and pay energy to emit into it. Moving Gaussian sources supply all energy; nothing is scripted. The same Rust engine runs the native command-line tool and this WebAssembly build.
